tag descriptions/uses -

<nav> - used for sections where there is internal site navigation - moving around on the current page, or for moving to other pages within the website.
should not be used for links to other websites

<article> - used for artciles within the website. These articles should hold up on their own, meaning that the content of
these pages taken outside of the context of the website should still make sense

<sections> - used for sections within the website that are clear and distinct, but wouldn't make sense outside of the scope
of the website. Usually used within artciles

<aside> - used for side notes/warnings that pertain to the information presented in the article, but aren't as significant
to the content of the article as sections

semantic tags - used for screen readers + other accessibility related devices

<header> - Used to introduce sections/articles/divs that follow. Not limited to one header per page, although I think you only want to use one. 

<footer> - Used for extra information that you might need. Copyright data, links to privacy pages, address, etc.

<main> - Used for the main part of the content of the page. Surrounds articles, sections, divs. Used for screen readers and search engines to analyze content of your page.
